Why LEAN365

Engage. Retain. Reward.

A practical program built for the way restaurants really run — energizing Team Members, keeping them longer, and rewarding their growth.

Engage

Engage and energize Team Members with video-based tutorials, high-energy activities, and creative challenges that make learning stick.

Retain

Team Members who participate in LEAN365 Essentials are more likely to stay — with a 70% retention rate among participants.

Reward

Team Members can earn College Credit for their participation as part of our partnership with Point University.

The program at a glance

Three phases that build Lean capability that lasts

A guided, 20-week journey — from developing yourself, to developing your team, to embedding continuous improvement into daily restaurant life.

Phase 1Weeks 1–8

Develop Self

The Lean Leader learns Lean tools and applies them to their own restaurant's performance.

  • Apply Lean tools and data to identify and eliminate waste
  • Prioritize and execute high-impact improvement opportunities
Phase 2Weeks 9–20

Develop Others

The Lean Leader coaches their team and runs structured improvement using LEAN365.

  • Inspire team engagement and commitment to continuous improvement
  • Lead change and leverage resources to sustain results
Phase 3Ongoing

Lean Culture & Value

Lean becomes part of how the restaurant runs: sustained, recognized, and continuously improving.

  • Reward, recognize and celebrate Lean champions and behaviors
  • Model and champion Lean leadership through consistent example

How the pilot works

From first interest to program kickoff

A simple, supported path — six steps from expressing interest to launching with your cohort.

1

Express Interest

Request to join the pilot, or connect through CFA outreach.

2

Get Selected

Your restaurant is accepted and expectations are confirmed.

3

Name a Lean Leader

Choose a team member to lead the work.

4

Get Set Up

Training vendor matched; tools and access prepared.

5

Prepare to Launch

Cohort confirmed; welcome and logistics shared.

6

Program Kicks Off

Your Lean Leader begins the program with the cohort.

Throughout the process, CFA and the training team provide support — Operators are never on their own.

What's included

Everything you need for a transformational LEAN journey

The program includes both digital and physical assets to engage Team Members. Every Team Member gets access to an online learning portal, and every restaurant receives the physical assets needed to launch.

  • Video TutorialsLEAN concepts with real restaurant examples.
  • Challenge CardsDone solo or as a team within each unit.
  • Games & ActivitiesReinforce content in a fun, engaging way.
  • Resources & ToolsApply LEAN concepts immediately in the restaurant.
  • Leader GuideEquips LEAN Leaders to facilitate the journey.
  • Promotional MaterialsPosters, prizes and giveaways to energize teams.
